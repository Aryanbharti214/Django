About SOA University

Siksha 'O' Anusandhan (SOA) is a private deemed-to-be university established in 1996. It offers programs in Engineering, Medical Sciences, Management, Law, Pharmacy, and more.

Constituent Institutes

  • ITER – Engineering
  • IMS & SUM Hospital – Medical
  • School of Management
  • School of Law
  • School of Pharmaceutical Sciences
